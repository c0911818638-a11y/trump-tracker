## 原文
Trump posted image of "The White House Accord on Super Intelligence" — subtitled "Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities" — signed by President Trump and tech executives: Sundar Pichai (Google), Elon Musk (Tesla/SpaceX), Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Mark Zuckerberg (Meta), Greg Brockman (OpenAI), Jensen Huang (Nvidia).

The accord states its signatories "believe that every company is responsible for developing its own technology safely and in a way that builds trust." Trump described it as "morally binding."

Also signed Executive Order "INAUGURATING THE ERA OF SUPER INTELLIGENCE" requiring federal agencies to replace "artificial intelligence"/"AI" with "Super Intelligence"/"SI" in all non-statutory documents, and directing officials to propose new legislative definitions within 60 days.
