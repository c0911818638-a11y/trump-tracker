## 原文

I am pleased to announce the nomination of Philip Aubart to the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina. Phil is currently the First Assistant U.S. Attorney in the Eastern District of North Carolina, where he previously served in the Office's Violent Crimes Section, and is also a member of the U.S. Army JAG Corps. He is a graduate of Dartmouth College and Duke University School of Law, and previously clerked for Judge Terrence Boyle on the Eastern District of North Carolina. Aubart has earned the backing of both North Carolina's Senators, Ted Budd and Thom Tillis, and is strongly recommended by Republican Candidate for United States Senate and former Head of the Republican Party, Michael Whatley.
