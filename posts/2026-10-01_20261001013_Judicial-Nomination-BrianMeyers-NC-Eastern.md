## 原文

I am pleased to announce the nomination of Brian Meyers to the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina. Currently a Magistrate Judge on that same Court, Brian previously served as an Assistant U.S. Attorney for more than a decade in the Eastern District of North Carolina and as an attorney in the U.S. Marine Corps. Brian graduated from Davidson College and the University of Richmond School of Law. Before earning his Law Degree, Brian worked for the late Senator Jesse Helms. The nomination has the strong support of both of North Carolina's Senators, Ted Budd and Thom Tillis, and Republican Candidate for United States Senate, and former Head of the Republican Party, Michael Whatley.
